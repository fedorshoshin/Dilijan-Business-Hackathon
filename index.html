Three ways to help

Pick one or all three. You can change it any time in the app.

  • Report a spot

    Take a photo, drop a pin and set what the cleanup pays. Then check the work when it is done.

  • Clean it up

    Pick a job near you, clean it, add an after photo. You are paid as soon as the reporter confirms.

  • Fund the work

    Give to the pot or to one spot, and see exactly which cleanups your money paid for, with before and after photos.

How a cleanup works

  1. Reported. Someone photographs a polluted spot and puts it on the map.
  2. Taken on. A cleaner claims it, so nobody else turns up for the same job.
  3. Cleaned. The cleaner adds a photo of the clean spot.
  4. Confirmed and paid. The reporter compares the photos and rates the work, and the cleaner is paid from donations.

Put it on your home screen

It opens like any other app, full screen, even with a weak signal.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open the app.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap Open the app.
  2. Tap Install when it is offered, or open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
